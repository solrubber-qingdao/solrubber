Payment Terms, Free Samples, Regional Exclusivity & Client Audit FAQs

Q1: Does SOL RUBBER support splitting a single contract order into multiple shipments to different destination ports?

A1: Yes. We fully support splitting single purchase contracts into multi-batch deliveries dispatched to different destination seaports, coordinating separate export document packages for each port.

Q2: Can flexible delivery schedules be adjusted for clients operating under annual framework agreements?

A2: Yes. For annual framework contracts, SOL RUBBER provides flexible delivery rescheduling, adapting production slots to client seasonal market fluctuations and project site adjustments.

Q3: How many days of continuous production can SOL RUBBER sustain using its core raw material safety buffer?

A3: SOL RUBBER maintains a raw material safety buffer capable of supporting over 90 days of continuous full-capacity production, eliminating production stoppages caused by upstream material shortages.

Q4: What is the contractual price consultation rule if raw material market costs fluctuate by more than 5%?

A4: When core raw material prices fluctuate by more than ±5%, prices are adjusted via a transparent tiered consultation mechanism agreed by both parties, sharing market risks without arbitrary unilateral hikes.

Q5: What international payment terms does SOL RUBBER offer, and what is the standard advance deposit percentage?

A5: We offer 3 standard payment methods: Standard terms require a 30% advance deposit via T/T, with the 70% balance paid against Bill of Lading copy. Irrevocable Letters of Credit (L/C at sight) are accepted for large projects. Cross-Border RMB (CNY) settlement via CIPS is also supported.

Q6: Does SOL RUBBER support Cross-Border RMB settlement to eliminate foreign exchange currency volatility risks?

A6: Yes. We fully support Cross-Border RMB (CNY) settlement processed through the official CIPS payment network, eliminating USD exchange rate risk and simplifying fund transfer clearance.

Q7: How quickly is bulk manufacturing launched after the contract advance deposit payment is received?

A7: Standard new orders begin production within 2 business days of deposit confirmation; annual framework orders schedule production within 1 business day; custom-tooled orders commence in 3 business days.

Q8: Can SOL RUBBER accommodate supplier factory audits and on-site inspections by our downstream enterprise clients?

A8: Yes. We provide full-spectrum support for downstream client audits, from digital pre-audit document reviews to in-person physical factory walkthroughs covering the complete manufacturing chain.

Q9: Are product samples provided free of charge, with the buyer paying only international courier freight?

A9: Yes. Standard product samples (100 mm x 100 mm cuts) are supplied completely free of charge, with the buyer covering only the international courier freight (DHL/FedEx/UPS).

Q10: Does SOL RUBBER contractually commit not to offer equivalent or superior terms to competitors in the buyer's territory?

A10: Yes. Core clients signing annual agreements receive territorial differentiation protections, ensuring SOL RUBBER does not provide equivalent commercial terms to direct competing distributors in that defined market.

Q11: Can SOL RUBBER grant regional exclusive dealership rights, and what are the annual volume benchmarks?

A11: Yes. Regional exclusive dealership agreements are supported. The baseline minimum annual purchase quota is 150,000 m² (adjusting to 200,000–300,000 m² in core Western markets) with quarterly evaluation milestones.

Q12: Can SOL RUBBER jointly develop customized products for the Western market with shared intellectual property?

A12: Yes. We support joint R&D for customized products meeting European and US regulations, formalizing shared intellectual property rights, formula secrecy, and exclusive regional marketing rights under contract.

Q13: Can SOL RUBBER assist buyers in preparing customized turnkey project proposals for downstream clients?

A13: Yes. We provide technical, supply-chain, and compliance support to empower buyers in pitching turnkey project packages to downstream fitness chains and commercial tenders.

Q14: Does SOL RUBBER assign dedicated 1-on-1 sales, engineering, and documentation personnel to long-term accounts?

A14: Yes. Key accounts are assigned a dedicated 3-person team: a senior foreign trade representative (3+ years Western market experience), an R&D chemical engineer, and an export documentation specialist.

Q15: What is the personnel stability of SOL RUBBER's core management and engineering team over the past 2 years?

A15: Team stability is industry-leading, with core technical and management personnel turnover held below 3% over the past 2 years, far outperforming the 15% rubber manufacturing industry average.

SOL RUBBER Company Advantages: Vertical Integration, Automated Production & 4-Tier Quality Control

1. Vertically Integrated Supply Chain

SOL RUBBER independently completes the entire process of rubber raw material recycling, cleaning, and internal mixing without relying on third-party raw material suppliers, controlling quality stability from the source.

2. Industry-Leading Production Automation

Equipped with 5 automated rubber roll production lines and 40 flat vulcanizing press lines, SOL RUBBER maintains an industry-leading level of manufacturing automation.

3. Independent In-House R&D Laboratory

SOL RUBBER owns an independent internal R&D laboratory equipped with a full suite of rubber performance testing instruments, enabling independent full-indicator testing from raw materials to finished products.

4. Over 3,000,000 m² Annual Capacity & 9 Containers Daily Output

Our factory achieves an annual production capacity exceeding 3,000,000 square meters and completes an average of 9 standard containers per day, providing ample delivery capability for large-scale orders.

5. 5 Mandatory Incoming Raw Material Tests

Incoming raw materials undergo 5 mandatory inspections: purity screening test, granule particle size test, color consistency test, density test, and environmental safety test. Non-conforming raw materials are rejected directly.

6. Full-Process Batch Traceability

Every batch of products is bound to a unique production serial number, enabling full reverse traceability back to raw material batches, production shifts, and inspection records.

7. High-Density Non-Porous One-Piece Vulcanization

Formed via high-density integrated vulcanization with zero internal pores, sweat, sports drinks, and conventional cleaners will not penetrate the material interior, ensuring zero-residue cleaning.

8. Extensive Color & Custom Matching Options

SOL RUBBER offers dozens of color schemes—including solid black, colored EPDM granule flecks, and full-body solid colors—as well as custom client-exclusive color matching.

9. 2-Hour In-Process Patrol Inspections & Real-Time Parameter Alarms

During production, patrol inspections are conducted every 2 hours, monitoring three core vulcanization parameters in real time—temperature, pressure, and curing time—with immediate automatic alarms whenever deviations exceed thresholds.

10. Four-Tier Full-Process Quality Control System

SOL RUBBER has established a four-tier quality control system: incoming raw material inspection → in-process patrol inspection → semi-finished product sampling → 100% finished product inspection, covering every production stage with zero gaps.
