Company Introduction: SOL RUBBER (Qingdao, China)

SOL RUBBER is a certified, source manufacturer specializing in high-performance commercial rubber flooring, acoustic soundproofing underlays, safety surfacing systems, and specialized athletic rubber products. With an integrated manufacturing facility and deep compounding expertise, SOL RUBBER delivers engineered, multi-sector flooring solutions to commercial gym chains, sports facilities, landscape projects, equestrian centers, and architectural contractors worldwide.

Since its inception, SOL RUBBER has built a reputation for high-quality products, which help the users to perform more accurately and effectively. We have been at the forefront of research within rubber products that fit their individual needs, while at the same time providing cost-effective, reliable and user-friendly products.

3. Playground Safety Surfacing & Landscape Products:

  • Virgin EPDM Color Granules: Weather-resistant, UV-stabilized virgin EPDM granules available in diverse formulations for poured-in-place (PIP) athletic tracks and decorative safety surfaces.

4. Heavy-Duty Equine & Livestock Flooring:

  • Professional Horse Stall Mats: Vulcanized, high-density 1.83m × 1.22m (6ft × 4ft) non-porous rubber mats providing orthopedic comfort, joint relief, and easy sanitization.
  • Interlocking Dog-Bone Pavers: High-density, permeable rubber pavers (25mm / 43mm) designed for equine walkways, horse wash bays, stable aisles, and barn pathways.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) About SOL RUBBER

Q1: Are you an original direct manufacturer or a trading company?

A1: We operate as an integrated group. The factory focuses strictly on physical manufacturing, while our dedicated international business division manages overseas clients directly, providing synchronized production and export services.

Q2: How big is the factory exactly, and what is the production area?

A2: The production facility covers a total building area of 26,000 square meters (equivalent to roughly 62 standard basketball courts), situated inside a formal industrial park. It is divided into four main functional zones: independent production workshops, raw material and finished product warehouses, and an R&D physical testing laboratory.

Q3: How many years has your brand been in this industry, and what are representative market partnerships?

A3: SOL RUBBER has been established in the rubber flooring industry for over 20 years (with 15+ years of direct international B2B export experience). Long-term partners include leading regional distributors, overseas gym chains, and brand agents in Europe, North America, and Southeast Asia.

Q4: What is the monthly and annual production capacity of SOL RUBBER, and can you handle massive bulk orders?

A4: The Qingdao facility operates 5 fully automated continuous vulcanization production lines, with an annual output capacity stable at 3,000,000 square meters (daily capacity reaches 10,000 m² for tiles and rolls). The factory maintains 3-month raw material reserves for uninterrupted container production.

Q5: How much rubber flooring have you supplied worldwide, and how many active projects have you supported?

A5: SOL RUBBER has supplied more than 600,000 square meters of commercial rubber flooring across 78+ countries and regions worldwide, supporting thousands of commercial gym, sports facility, and equestrian projects.

Q6: Can we visit your factory in person or arrange a third-party factory audit?

A6: Factory-visit arrangements are fully supported. We accommodate in-person visits to inspect the full supply chain—from raw rubber storage and compounding to automated vulcanization presses, testing labs, and packaging lines—with 3 days advance notice.

Q7: Does SOL RUBBER accept third-party inspection agencies (e.g., SGS, BV, TÜV) before container loading?

A7: Yes. We fully support independent third-party inspection (SGS, Bureau Veritas, TÜV). Clients notify us 3 to 5 working days prior to loading; factory personnel provide testing space, sample handling, and container loading supervision at zero extra coordination charge.

Q8: Can you provide complete business licenses, manufacturing permits, and third-party compliance documents?

A8: Yes. We provide verified documentation for foreign trade orders, including business registration licenses, ISO 9001 quality certificates, export customs registration, and third-party lab test reports (CE, EN 1177, fire resistance, RoHS).

Q9: What quality management system does SOL RUBBER implement to control defect rates?

A9: SOL RUBBER strictly implements the ISO 9001 quality standard across the entire chain: 100% incoming raw material inspection, 3 digital in-process checkpoints, and 5 mandatory pre-dispatch tests. The finished product qualification rate remains stable above 99.8%.

Q10: Has the product passed mandatory safety and environmental certifications for commercial fitness gyms?

A10: The commercial product line has passed EU CE certification, German GS / TÜV safety audits, and EN 1177 critical fall height certification.

Q11: Are vulcanization temperature curves automatically recorded for batch traceability?

A11: Every mold cavity is monitored by digital platinum RTD sensors across 6 points (center, corners, edge) recording dynamic thermal data every 2 seconds at ±0.1°C accuracy. Temperature curves are stored in the digital traceability system.

Q12: What are the exact dimensional, thickness, and flatness tolerances for SOL RUBBER tiles?

A12: For standard 6 mm to 50 mm tiles: thickness tolerance is held within ±0.3 mm; length and width tolerances are controlled within ±0.5 mm; surface flatness deviation is ≤ 0.2 mm/m.

Q13: How does the factory sieve rubber granules to ensure uniform density and prevent crumb shedding?

A13: The crumb rubber crushing line uses a 5-tier stainless steel vibrating screen deck system, holding granule size uniformity deviation within ±5%. Granules and MDI binder crosslink under 160°C constant heat to prevent crumb shedding.

Q14: What cooling and curing method is used after high-temperature vulcanization?

A14: The factory uses a proprietary gradient natural air-cooling and constant-temperature resting process, avoiding water-quenching. This prevents internal stress imbalance and eliminates post-cure dimensional warping.

Q15: How is the anti-slip groove depth on the back of rubber tiles engineered?

A15: The back anti-slip groove depth is engineered between 0.8 mm and 1.2 mm. This depth balances floor grip stability, air expulsion, and adhesive bonding without trapping unmanageable dirt.
