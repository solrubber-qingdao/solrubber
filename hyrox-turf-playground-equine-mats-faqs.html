1. Composite EPDM/SBR Flooring & HYROX Functional Sled Turf

  • Virgin EPDM + Recycled SBR Composite: Heat-vulcanized 100% virgin EPDM colored wear layers bonded to dense recycled SBR cores deliver UV abrasion resistance alongside shock absorption.
  • Non-Infill PE Sled Turf & HYROX Specs: Because bare vulcanized rubber creates excessive friction on metal sled skis, sled and sprint tracks use high-density texturized polyethylene (PE) turf without rubber infill—including competition-grade HYROX turf (gauge 3/8", 16 mm pile height) engineered for sled pushes, lunges, and burpees.
  • Rubber Shockpad Integration: Specialized gym turf bonds directly over vulcanized rubber underlayment via PU turf adhesive for integrated sprint and sled deceleration.

2. EN 1177 Playground Safety Tiles, HIC Testing & Stormwater Drainage

  • Critical Fall Height (CFH) & HIC ≤ 1,000: Head Injury Criterion (HIC) evaluates metal headform impact deceleration; EN 1177 requires HIC ≤ 1,000 at rated fall heights: 45 mm (1.4 m CFH), 50 mm (1.5 m CFH), and 100 mm (3.0 m CFH), verified by SGS/TÜV EN 1177 and ASTM F1292 lab reports.
  • Kindergarten Skin-Contact Safety: Passes REACH SVHC 204 chemical screening and EN 71-3 toy safety limits with zero lead or cadmium.
  • Subbase, Drainage & Waste Allowance: Porous coarse granules and grooved underside pedestals drain stormwater along subbase slopes. Never install directly over grass or bare soil—prepare a 150 mm compacted crushed stone base with edge restraints, measure ramp linear meters, and add 10%–12% cutting scrap for irregular layouts.

How are SOL RUBBER playground tile thicknesses matched to EN 1177 Critical Fall Height and HIC ≤ 1,000?

Under EN 1177, Critical Fall Height (CFH) is the maximum drop height where Head Injury Criterion (HIC) stays ≤ 1,000. Thickness maps directly to CFH: 45 mm for 1.4 m CFH, 50 mm for 1.5 m CFH, and 100 mm for 3.0 m CFH, backed by SGS/TÜV EN 1177 and ASTM F1292 reports.
