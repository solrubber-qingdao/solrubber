Export Rights, Customs Compliance, OEM Packaging & Mega-Order FAQs

Q1: Can SOL RUBBER assist in extending marine insurance coverage to include piracy and war risks?

A1: Yes. We handle full-policy extensions to include piracy and war risks under standard marine insurance addendum clauses, eliminating the need for buyers to negotiate with underwriters directly.

Q2: Can SOL RUBBER coordinate all documentation required to process a marine insurance total-loss claim?

A2: Yes. We coordinate and issue all core documents: matched Commercial Invoices, Packing Lists, Contracts, Bills of Lading, Certificate of Origin, mill batch certificates, and damage assessment statements.

Q3: Can SOL RUBBER supply complete technical dossiers to clear random destination customs audits?

A3: Yes. We supply full technical compliance documentation to resolve non-security related technical queries arising from random destination customs inspections, minimizing cargo retention times.

Q4: Can SOL RUBBER issue FORM E Certificates of Origin to qualify for zero tariffs in ASEAN markets?

A4: Yes. SOL RUBBER issues official government-stamped FORM E Certificates of Origin, enabling qualifying buyers to claim preferential zero-tariff or reduced-tariff treatment across ASEAN member nations.

Q5: Does export packaging contain zero sensitive or non-compliant markings that could trigger customs seizure?

A5: Yes. Outer packaging undergoes strict compliance management. Packaging contains zero restricted, unapproved, or politically sensitive designs or text, preventing destination customs detentions.

Q6: Can SOL RUBBER assist in arranging destination drayage trucking from port to overseas warehouse?

A6: Yes. Leveraging our global freight network, we assist buyers in coordinating destination local drayage trucking from port terminal to overseas warehouses at competitive local logistics rates.

Q7: Does SOL RUBBER provide full-process container loading photographs for buyer records?

A7: Yes. We provide comprehensive photo sets documenting cargo packaging, container empty condition, loading progression, pallet placement, cargo securing, and the sealed door container seal number.

Q8: Does SOL RUBBER hold independent self-operated import and export rights for direct customs clearance?

A8: Yes. SOL RUBBER possesses full legal self-operated import/export rights, directly processing export customs declarations without relying on third-party brokerages, with over 3 years of direct EU/US export track records.

Q9: Does SOL RUBBER have at least 3 years of actual supply experience exporting to Western markets?

A9: Yes. SOL RUBBER officially launched direct export operations in 2007 (approaching 20 years of continuous global export history), with North America and Western Europe serving as core destination markets.

Q10: Does SOL RUBBER support printing custom buyer brand logos and patterns directly on tile surfaces?

A10: Yes. We fully support printing client proprietary brand logos or graphic patterns on tile surfaces, utilizing UV flatbed printing, silk-screen printing, or heat-vulcanized mold markings.

Q11: What packaging design is used to prevent edge and corner impact damage during international transit?

A11: Multi-layer reinforced packaging: 1. Heavy corrugated cardboard edge protectors on all 4 corners. 2. 5-layer stretch film wrap. 3. 5-ply solid timber top board. 4. Four heavy-duty strapping bands securing pallets. Over 99% of transit damage is prevented.

Q12: Can master cartons be printed with buyer-specified shipping marks for automated overseas warehousing?

A12: Yes. Buyers provide vector artwork; we issue digital proofs before printing master cartons with custom layout, SKU barcodes, pallet numbers, and batch details to streamline overseas warehouse scanning.

Q13: Can all factory brand markings be fully eliminated from products and packaging for transit trade?

A13: Yes. We support full-chain neutral packaging. All factory names, supplier logos, Chinese characters, and origin identifiers are completely removed from products and cartons for international re-distribution.

Q14: Has SOL RUBBER executed mega-orders exceeding 100,000 m² over the past 2 years, and what was the delivery record?

A14: Yes. In the past 2 years, SOL RUBBER has successfully executed multiple single orders exceeding 100,000 square meters, achieving a 100% on-time delivery record with zero delays and zero batch rejections.

Q15: How many days of free factory warehouse storage does SOL RUBBER provide after production completion?

A15: SOL RUBBER provides up to 30 calendar days of free warehouse storage for all export orders, calculated from the inspection completion date, giving buyers ample flexibility to schedule vessel bookings.
