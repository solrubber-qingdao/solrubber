NDAs, Sinosure OA Credit, GSP Form A & Glue-Free Interlocking Mat FAQs

Q1: Does SOL RUBBER contractually commit never to supply buyer-customized proprietary products to direct competitors?

A1: Yes. Proprietary customized tiles, molds, and formulas are recorded under exclusive archives, backed by formal non-compete clauses prohibiting supply to designated direct competitors.

Q2: Does SOL RUBBER strictly execute non-disclosure agreements regarding client pricing, volumes, and tender lists?

A2: Yes. All commercial data—including downstream client rosters, tender bids, customized recipes, and annual volumes—is protected under binding bilateral Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs).

Q3: How short can manufacturing lead times be compressed for critical emergency replacement orders?

A3: In-stock inventory ships within 1 week (reaching departure berths in 5 days); standard general tiles under 10,000 m² ship within 10 days; small-batch custom colors compress to 15 days.

Q4: Are standard export palletization, corner guards, and carton packaging costs included in the quoted price?

A4: Yes. Factory standard commercial quotations include all international export-grade packaging costs (fumigated/plywood pallets, corner protectors, and master cartons) without hidden surcharges.

Q5: How many days is a formal quotation from SOL RUBBER typically valid for core partners?

A5: Official commercial quotations for long-term core partners remain valid for approximately 90 days, significantly exceeding the foreign trade industry average of 30 days.

Q6: Which international standard payment terms are supported by SOL RUBBER?

A6: Standard terms: 30% advance deposit via T/T, with the 70% balance paid against Bill of Lading copy. Large commercial engineering contracts accept Irrevocable Letters of Credit (L/C at sight); sample orders accept 100% advance T/T.

Q7: Does SOL RUBBER accept payment terms of 30% advance deposit and 70% balance against B/L copy?

A7: Yes. SOL RUBBER fully accepts 30% advance deposit via T/T and 70% balance payment against the copy of the original Ocean Bill of Lading as our standard international commercial payment structure.

Q8: Can SOL RUBBER provide Open Account (OA) credit terms for long-term wholesale partners?

A8: Yes. For core partners with over 3 years of partnership and zero payment default records, limited OA credit lines can be extended, subject to credit approval by China Export & Credit Insurance Corporation (Sinosure).

Q9: What specific compliance criteria are required to qualify for SOL RUBBER's OA credit terms?

A9: Requirements: 1. Continuous cooperation for ≥ 36 months with zero payment defaults. 2. Annual purchase volume of at least US$500,000. 3. Downstream buyer credit approved at Grade A or higher under Sinosure evaluation.

Q10: Can SOL RUBBER provide official GSP Certificates of Origin for applicable beneficiary nations?

A10: Yes. For qualifying export destinations that maintain GSP trade preferences, SOL RUBBER issues official Generalized System of Preferences Certificates of Origin (GSP Form A) to support tariff-free entry.

Q11: Is supplementary adhesive required when installing SOL RUBBER flooring products?

A11: Adhesive depends on installation type: Loose-lay uses tile self-weight (no adhesive required); permanent high-traffic commercial installations require full-spread polyurethane adhesive over flat concrete subfloors.

Q12: Can interlocking rubber gym tiles achieve 100% adhesive-free loose-laid installation?

A12: Yes. Interlocking puzzle tiles achieve 100% glue-free installation without any adhesive, representing our most mature glue-free commercial gym flooring design.

Q13: What subfloor conditions are unsuitable for adhesive-free interlocking rubber tile installation?

A13: Unsuitable conditions: Subfloor flatness gap > 3 mm under a 2 m straightedge; dusty/uncured slabs; moisture content > 8%; plush carpet/spongy foam bases; uncured epoxy slabs; heavy forklift zones; or outdoor temperatures below -10°C.

Q14: Is SOL RUBBER flooring specifically engineered for cardio treadmill and stationary bike zones?

A14: Yes. Flooring for cardio zones is engineered to resist continuous machine wheel pressure, dampen motor vibrations, reduce mechanical hum, and provide wet slip resistance against sweat.

Q15: Does SOL RUBBER manufacture dedicated thick rubber tiles engineered for Olympic weightlifting platforms?

A15: Yes. We manufacture heavy-duty Olympic lifting platform tiles in thicknesses up to 50 mm, engineered specifically to absorb the kinetic shock of 20 kg+ barbells dropped from 1.8 m.
