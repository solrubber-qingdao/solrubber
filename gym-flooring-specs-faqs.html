Technical Performance, Product Portfolio & Customization FAQs

Q1: What is the machining tolerance of interlocking pin and puzzle joints, and what is the installed seam gap?

A1: Interlocking joints are machined to a precision tolerance of ±0.3 mm. When properly assembled, the installed seam gap between adjacent tiles is narrower than 0.3 mm, providing a seamless visual surface.

Q2: What static weight load can SOL RUBBER tiles support in heavy free-weight gym areas?

A2: High-density vulcanized rubber tiles of 30 mm thickness and above can support continuous static loads of 2.0 metric tons per square meter (2,000 kg/m ²) over concrete subfloors without permanent structural depression.

Q3: Can the rubber gym tiles withstand repetitive drops of a 25 kg dumbbell from a 1.2 m height?

A3: Yes. Dedicated free-weight tiles withstand repetitive impact drops of a 25 kg iron dumbbell from a 1.2 m height without cracking, gouging, structural rupture, or subfloor concrete damage.

Q4: What is the certified impact shock-absorption percentage across different tile thicknesses?

A4: Shock absorption ratings: 8–15 mm tiles achieve 30%–40% shock absorption; 20–30 mm tiles achieve 45%–55% shock absorption; 40–50 mm heavy-drop tiles achieve 60%–80% kinetic shock absorption.

Q5: What are the dynamic friction coefficient ratings under dry and wet floor conditions?

A5: Tested per commercial safety standards: the dry dynamic coefficient of friction is ≥ 0.5 (typically 0.6–0.8); the wet dynamic coefficient of friction remains ≥ 0.3 (typically ≥ 0.6 on textured wear surfaces).

Q6: What fire classification does SOL RUBBER achieve, and does it release toxic halogen gases when burned?

A6: Flooring achieves China GB 8624 Class B1 and European EN 13501-1 Class Bfl-s1. It extinguishes rapidly once flame is removed, with low smoke toxicity and zero toxic halogen acid gases (unlike PVC).

Q7: Does the flooring have an offensive chemical odor, and how long does initial dissipation take?

A7: Flooring uses non-toxic MDI binder with zero recycled scrap shoe rubber. It emits only a light, natural vulcanized rubber scent upon unpacking, which dissipates within 3 to 7 days with normal room ventilation.

Q8: Does the polyurethane binder contain toxic solvents, benzene, or free formaldehyde?

A8: The binder is 100% solvent-free polymer MDI prepolymer. It contains no formaldehyde, benzene, toluene, xylene, or heavy metal catalyst additives, meeting indoor E1 emission limits.

Q9: Does SOL RUBBER participate in international fitness industry trade exhibitions?

A9: Yes. SOL RUBBER actively participates in leading international trade shows, regularly exhibiting at FIBO Cologne in Germany (the world's largest fitness trade show) and the China Sport Show.

Q10: What is the customer repurchase rate, and how are new clients acquired?

A10: Core customer repurchase rate remains stable above 75% (industry average is around 30%). Over 60% of new annual clients are direct referrals from existing commercial partners.

Q11: What are the core product categories manufactured by SOL RUBBER for commercial gyms?

A11: The core gym flooring portfolio comprises four primary categories: Composite Gym Rubber Tiles, Interlocking Gym Rubber Mats, EPDM Colorful Rubber Gym Flooring, and Commercial High-Impact Rubber Rolls.

Q12: Which major global markets and regions does SOL RUBBER primarily supply?

A12: SOL RUBBER exports to 8 global regions: Core markets are North America and Western Europe (certified by CE and EN 1177 for commercial gym chains); expanding markets include South America, Eastern Europe, and Southeast Asia; regional coverage extends across Oceania (Australia), the Middle East (Saudi Arabia), and Africa.

Q13: What is the standard thickness range manufactured, and how does thickness correspond to gym zones?

A13: Standard thicknesses range from 8 mm to 50 mm: 8–12 mm rolls (10 m x 1 m) suit cardio and open areas (loading 1,500–1,700 m² per 20'GP); 15–30 mm tiles suit functional and personal training zones; 40–50 mm heavy tiles suit heavy free-weight drop areas. Custom thicknesses beyond 50 mm are supported.

Q14: What color options and customization configurations are available for SOL RUBBER flooring?

A14: Options include: Basic Solid Black (100% SBR base for heavy weight areas); Black with EPDM Flecks (classic multi-zone gym style); 8 Solid Roll Colors (Red, Orange, Yellow, Green, Blue, Purple, White); Deep Customization (10% to 100% full-ratio EPDM flecks, custom brand VI Pantone matching, and surface UV logo printing).

Q15: What is the standard thickness specified for dedicated heavy free-weight lifting zones?

A15: Standard thickness for heavy-duty weight areas is 20 mm to 50 mm, with 50 mm tiles serving as the primary commercial specification. High-density SBR absorbs dropped barbells and dumbbells from 1.8 m heights without subfloor damage while cutting impact noise.
