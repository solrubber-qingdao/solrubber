1. Format Selection, Weight Engineering & Surface Tooling

  • 50 cm vs. 1 m Tile Comparison: Standard 500x500 mm (50x50 cm) tiles minimize cutting scrap in compact rooms, while 1,000x1,000 mm (1x1 m) tiles cut total visible seam meterage by 50% across large open gyms for faster installation and superior floor stability.
  • Exact Tile Weight Calculation: Calculated at 1.8 kg per mm thickness per m²—a 500x500x20 mm tile weighs 9.0 kg and a 1,000x1,000x20 mm tile weighs 36.0 kg.
  • Zone-Matched Thicknesses & Non-Standard 35 mm: Heavy free-weight zones use dense 20–50 mm tiles (including specialized 35 mm tiles), while functional/cardio areas use 8–15 mm seamless rolls or turf. Standard roll gauges are 6 mm, 8 mm, and 10 mm (2–12 mm range available) in 1 m or 1.22 m widths.
  • Smooth Rolls & Custom Steel Embossing: Pure-color smooth rubber rolls without flecks serve studios and physiotherapy clinics, while precision steel tooling creates proprietary diamond plate, hammer top, and pebble surface textures.
