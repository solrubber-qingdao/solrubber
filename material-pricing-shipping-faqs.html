Raw Materials, Environmental Compliance, Pricing & Export Logistics FAQs

Q1: What is the exact raw material ratio between recycled rubber and virgin rubber in SOL RUBBER products?

A1: The base core is composed of >90% recycled commercial SBR tire rubber granules (cleaned, desulfurized, and sieved). The top surface layer is composed of 100% virgin EPDM rubber granules (zero recycled scrap). Overall finished product composition contains approximately 80% to 85% recycled rubber, balancing structural shock absorption with high wear resistance.

Q2: Are colored EPDM granules integrated into the flooring, and how are they formulated?

A2: Yes. Formulations include: 100% pure virgin EPDM surface wear layers (pure color, zero recycled scrap, friction ≥ 0.6); Black SBR flecked with EPDM colored granules (classic commercial gym design); and 100% solid EPDM custom colors. All EPDM granules comply with European CE environmental standards with non-detectable heavy metals and VOCs.

Q3: Do SOL RUBBER products comply with mandatory environmental standards for EU and North American markets?

A3: EU: Fully compliant with CE Construction Products Regulation (fire resistance, formaldehyde, hazardous substance limits), REACH Regulation (all 204 SVHC substances below 0.1% threshold), and RoHS Directive heavy metal limits. North America: Complies with commercial indoor floor air quality limits for formaldehyde and VOC emissions, exported to over 80 countries.

Q4: Can SOL RUBBER assist buyers in calculating total landed cost to Western ports including import duties?

A4: Yes. We provide free comprehensive landed-cost calculation sheets, detailing factory product cost, ocean freight, destination port terminal handling charges, estimated customs import tariffs, and local trucking fees, allowing buyers to budget accurate procurement costs.

Q5: What is the maximum annual price increase percentage guaranteed by SOL RUBBER?

A5: SOL RUBBER contractually guarantees that annual regular price increases will not exceed 5% (well below the 10% industry average), protecting client local market pricing stability. In the event of extreme raw material cost spikes, a minimum of 1 month's advance written notice is provided.

Q6: Can buyers lock current purchase prices for 6 months to hedge against raw material price increases?

A6: Yes. By signing a 6-month purchasing framework agreement and depositing a 30% framework deposit, SOL RUBBER guarantees a 100% price lock on confirmed specifications, insulating buyers entirely from market cost surges in rubber and polyurethane binders.

Q7: What is the maximum price-lock period available for long-term wholesale partners?

A7: Strategic core partners with over 3 years of continuous collaboration can secure an extended price-lock term of up to 12 months, covering their full annual purchasing plan and eliminating frequent quote adjustments.

A8: For wholesale clients whose annual actual purchase volume exceeds their framework contract target by 100x20' containers, SOL RUBBER provides an annual rebate of 1 free container of goods, which can be applied to customer replacement stock or profit margins.

A9: After factoring in factory product price, ocean shipping, import tariffs, and logistics, total landed cost from SOL RUBBER is typically 28% to 35% lower than purchasing equivalent-specification commercial flooring manufactured locally in Europe or North America.

Q10: Does SOL RUBBER flooring fully comply with commercial gym athletic surface safety standards?

A10: Yes. Commercial-grade products achieve full certification across shock absorption, dynamic friction coefficients, and abrasion resistance, fulfilling all international building and gym surface safety criteria.

Q11: How quickly can standard warehouse stock orders be dispatched following payment confirmation?

A11: Standard ready-stock orders are packed, customs-declared, and scheduled for vessel loading within 7 working days following commercial payment confirmation, significantly outperforming the 15-day industry benchmark.

Q12: What is the standard manufacturing lead time for custom non-stock wholesale production orders?

A12: Standard manufacturing lead time for customized, non-stock wholesale orders is 20 to 25 calendar days, strictly managed across compounding, press scheduling, cutting, and QA inspection.

Q13: Does SOL RUBBER accept mixed Less-than-Container Load (LCL) consolidation orders to minimize trial costs?

A13: Yes. We fully support multi-SKU consolidations combining different thicknesses and colors in a single order, with a low minimum consolidation volume of only 20 cubic meters (CBM) to reduce trial risk for new buyers.

Q14: Can products of different thicknesses and colors be consolidated within the same ocean container?

A14: Yes. We support multi-SKU container loading, providing an engineered loading plan with distinct pallet zoning by batch and clear packing lists to simplify warehouse sorting upon arrival.

Q15: Does SOL RUBBER provide full-service export logistics covering factory trucking, booking, and customs?

A15: Yes. We provide complete one-stop door-to-port logistics services, handling factory drayage, container trucking, shipping line booking, export customs clearance, and terminal documentation; consignees simply receive cargo at their destination port.
