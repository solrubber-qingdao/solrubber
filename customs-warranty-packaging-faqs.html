Customs Documentation, Warranty, Trial Orders & Packaging FAQs

Q1: How many hours after vessel departure does SOL RUBBER provide the full set of Ocean Bill of Lading documents?

A1: Within 48 hours after vessel departure, SOL RUBBER provides scanned copies of the original Ocean Bill of Lading and all customs clearance documents, enabling buyers to initiate destination port clearance early.

Q2: Can SOL RUBBER provide Telex Release services for the Ocean Bill of Lading for urgent customs clearance?

A2: Yes. For urgent clearance orders, SOL RUBBER provides Telex Release services free of charge. Consignees clear cargo immediately upon arrival using the Telex copy, eliminating courier waiting time and port demurrage fees.

Q3: If customs clearance issues arise at the destination port, how quickly does SOL RUBBER provide supporting documents?

A3: SOL RUBBER maintains a dedicated customs support team that issues a complete set of supplementary compliance documents within 2 hours of notification, assisting buyers in expediting customs clearance.

Q4: Can SOL RUBBER arrange marine cargo insurance to protect buyers against transit damage risks, and what is the rate?

A4: Yes. We arrange All-Risks marine cargo insurance at a premium of only 0.15% of the commercial cargo value, covering total physical damage and loss from the factory warehouse to the destination warehouse.

Q5: What is the official global warranty period for SOL RUBBER commercial rubber flooring products?

A5: The official global warranty for all commercial-grade rubber flooring ranges from 1 to 3 years (with mainstream commercial series backed by 3 to 5 years), covering all non-human manufacturing defects.

Q6: What is the contractual compensation protocol if non-human quality defects occur during the warranty term?

A6: If non-human quality defects are confirmed during the warranty period, SOL RUBBER issues a 100% free replacement volume in the subsequent order, or provides a direct credit deduction against invoice value.

Q7: What is the guaranteed response time limit for technical and product quality complaints?

A7: SOL RUBBER guarantees an initial formal written response within 12 hours of complaint submission, and issues a fully documented technical resolution plan within 24 hours.

Q8: What is the compensation procedure if packaging or cargo arrives damaged during ocean transit?

A8: Provided All-Risks marine cargo insurance was arranged through us, 100% of packaging damage and product physical loss during transit is compensated in full by the insurance underwriter, with SOL RUBBER managing the claim filing.

Q9: Does SOL RUBBER support 100% neutral unbranded export packaging without factory identification?

A9: Yes. We fully support 100% neutral unbranded packaging. The carton exteriors and pallets contain zero factory names, factory logos, or origin marks, accommodating private-label distributors.

Q10: Does SOL RUBBER offer low-risk trial order arrangements for new wholesale buyers?

A10: Yes. New buyers can place trial orders with a low threshold of only 50 square meters, while enjoying wholesale bulk pricing and an additional 5% trial order discount.

Q11: Does newly installed SOL RUBBER flooring emit strong odors, and what is the dissipation period?

A11: New flooring emits no toxic chemical odors, carrying only a mild natural vulcanized rubber scent. Under standard ventilation, the scent fades substantially within 3 to 7 days (or within 3 days with fans).

Q12: Does SOL RUBBER flooring resist localized point-load indentation under spin bike feet?

A12: Yes. We recommend dense rubber flooring with thickness ≥ 10 mm (avoiding thin rolls under 6 mm) installed over a flat subfloor with full-spread adhesive; dense 8 mm+ vulcanized rubber dissipates pedal impact without denting.

Q13: Can SOL RUBBER provide origin certificates enabling US and European GSP duty-free treatment?

A13: SOL RUBBER provides official China Certificate of Origin documents (CO); however, due to US and EU bilateral GSP graduation policies regarding China, GSP duty-free status is governed by destination policy restrictions.

Q14: Does export packaging comply with international ocean wood packaging quarantine regulations without fumigation delays?

A14: Yes. Standard export packaging uses fumigation-free engineered wood pallets, or solid timber pallets pre-treated and heat-stamped under IPPC regulations, eliminating destination quarantine hold-ups and extra fumigation costs.

Q15: Do timber pallets bear official IPPC treatment stamps to prevent destination port customs retention?

A15: Yes. All solid timber pallets undergo statutory IPPC heat-treatment/fumigation and bear official IPPC markings, preventing destination customs from holding cargo due to quarantine non-compliance.
